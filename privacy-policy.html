Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aentigo ("Aentigo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Aentigo mobile application and related services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Aentigo, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: information used to create and manage your account, such as your name, username, email address, account identifier, and authentication information.
  • Profile information: information you choose to add to your profile, including a profile photo, display name, description, and other profile details.
  • User-generated content: posts, comments, messages, service requests, job-related content, reviews, reports, and other content you submit through Aentigo.
  • Media and files: images and audio that you choose to upload or record when using supported Aentigo features.
  • Service and activity information: information related to jobs, service requests, completed interactions, saved content, account preferences, blocking, reporting, and other activity within the app.
  • Technical information: information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as device and app information, authentication identifiers, diagnostic information, and notification tokens.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide and operate Aentigo's features.
  • Display profiles and user-generated content where appropriate.
  • Enable communication between users.
  • Provide job and service-related functionality.
  • Save content and user preferences.
  • Send service-related notifications.
  • Process reports and blocking actions.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, spam, and misuse.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of Aentigo.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Information shared with other users

Aentigo includes social and marketplace-style features. Information that you intentionally make available through your profile, posts, comments, reviews, job or service activity may be visible to other users as necessary to provide those features.

Messages and other communications may be visible to the users participating in the relevant conversation.

4. Service providers

Aentigo uses third-party infrastructure and technology providers to operate the service. In particular, Aentigo uses Google Firebase services for functions such as authentication, database storage, file storage, cloud functionality, and notifications.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security obligations.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Aentigo, maintain the security and integrity of the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

7. Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your Aentigo account from within the Aentigo app by opening Settings, selecting Delete Account, and confirming permanent deletion.

Account deletion removes personal account information and data associated exclusively with the account, including applicable profile information, private account data, uploaded profile images, posts, and saved content.

Some limited information may be retained or anonymized where necessary for safety, abuse and fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal obligations, reports, reviews, or shared transaction and service history.

More information about account deletion is available at: https://aentigo.com/delete-account.html

8. User choices and controls

Aentigo provides controls that allow users to manage certain account information and content. Users can also block other users, report inappropriate activity, and permanently delete their account.

9. Children's privacy

Aentigo is not intended for children under the minimum age permitted to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would be prohibited by applicable law.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices, or legal obligations change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact Aentigo through the support contact information provided by Aentigo.
