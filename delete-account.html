Delete your Aentigo account

Aentigo users can permanently delete their account and associated personal data directly from the Aentigo app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Aentigo app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm Delete permanently.

After confirmation, the account deletion process begins and the account can no longer be used.

Data that is deleted

Account deletion removes personal account data such as your profile, private account information, uploaded profile images, posts, saved content, and other data associated exclusively with your account.

Data that may be retained

Limited information may be retained or anonymized where necessary for safety, abuse and fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal obligations, reports, reviews, or shared transaction and service history.

Where appropriate, identifying information is removed or replaced so that shared records can remain without displaying the deleted user's personal identity.

Can't access the app?
If you cannot access your Aentigo account or need assistance with an account deletion request, please contact Aentigo support.
